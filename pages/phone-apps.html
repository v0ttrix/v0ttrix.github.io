A driving diary that uses your phone's motion sensors and GPS to track hard braking, rapid acceleration, sharp turns, and travel speed. Trips stay on your phone and contribute to a driving score, with a notification summarizing each trip. Planned features include route history, event markers, personal trends, and adjustable sensitivity.
