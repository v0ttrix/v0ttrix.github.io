ClariNexus Building Energy Analysis

PythonStreamlitPandasNumPyPlotlyScikit-learn

Overview

A Streamlit app for examining building energy use alongside outdoor temperature and occupancy. Users can upload time-series CSV data, adjust the temperature threshold, and compare energy patterns across occupied and unoccupied hours.

Features

  • Load and validate time-series energy datasets.
  • Use Pandas and NumPy for preparation and analysis.
  • Fit linear regression models with Scikit-learn.
  • Build interactive charts with Plotly.
  • Compare occupancy, weekdays, weekends, holidays, and monthly patterns.

Implementation Notes

The implementation separates analysis, chart generation, UI helpers, and configuration. Source code is private.
