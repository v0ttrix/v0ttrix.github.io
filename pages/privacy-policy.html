Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how this personal portfolio, operated by Abd Ulrahman (Jaden) Mardini, handles information when you browse the site, contact me, or use My 3D World.

Browsing and hosting

The portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. Browsing the portfolio does not require an account, and the portfolio code does not include advertising trackers or a visitor analytics service. GitHub receives requests needed to deliver the site and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

Information stored in your browser

The site uses local storage to remember your language choice and session storage to vary the homepage project preview. My 3D World also uses local storage for a randomly generated browser identifier, achievements, selected rewards, play progress, sound preference, and a country flag you choose. These settings are stored in your browser; they are not a registered user account. The portfolio code does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

You can clear this site's stored data in your browser settings. Doing so resets saved preferences and game progress and may create a new browser identifier. Clearing local data does not remove submissions already stored on the world server.

Optional public world features

The 3D world's scoreboard and whispers connect to a Cloudflare Worker. Race submissions send your browser identifier, three-character tag, chosen country flag, completion time, and checkpoint timings. The leaderboard displays tags, flags, and times. Whispers send your browser identifier, message, chosen flag, and position in the world; messages, flags, and positions are visible to other visitors. Please do not include sensitive or private information in public messages.

The server associates submissions with the browser identifier to update scores and whispers. It stores up to 100 race records for the current UTC day, with the top ten shown publicly; scores reset daily. It keeps up to 100 whispers, replacing a browser's previous whisper and removing older entries when the limit is reached. Whispers have no automatic time-based expiry. A shared lava interaction counter is also stored. These records may be removed during maintenance or moderation.

The Worker uses a hash of the connecting IP address for submission rate limits and connection handling; its application code does not persist the raw IP address. Hashing does not make an IP-derived identifier anonymous. Cloudflare processes network requests to operate and protect the service under its Privacy Policy. Hosting providers may process technical information outside your country; their retention practices are governed by their own policies.

Fonts in My 3D World

The world requests fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your browser's font requests and associated network information to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that information.

Contact and external links

Contact buttons open your email application or an external profile. If you email me, I receive the address, message, and attachments you send and use them to respond and manage our correspondence. Avoid sending sensitive information unnecessarily. I keep correspondence as needed for that purpose; there is no automatic deletion schedule stated for email.

Links to projects, social profiles, app stores, and client websites take you to independently operated services with their own terms and privacy practices. Clicking a link or using those services may share information with their operators.

Your choices and questions

You can browse without sending public world submissions, clear browser storage, and choose whether to follow external links. For questions or requests concerning information you submitted, email jaden.mardini@proton.me. Describe the relevant message, tag, and approximate date so I can identify it; do not send passwords. I will assess requests in light of the information available and applicable requirements. I cannot remove copies others may have saved or control hosting providers' independent records.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website's features change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
