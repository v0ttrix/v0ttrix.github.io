// About Me

I'm Abd Ulrahman Mardini, but I go by Jaden. I'm a computer science student at the University of Guelph, with a computer engineering background from Conestoga College.

I started with hardware: soldering, wiring, and figuring out why a circuit wasn't doing what I expected. These days I spend more time writing software and testing systems, but I still like projects where the code does something you can see. That includes a browser-controlled robot, a plant monitoring app, and a CTF platform running on a Raspberry Pi.

At LinePal, I tested the frontend, APIs, and backend, and documented 14 confirmed vulnerabilities. At ClariNexus, I worked on data pipelines and application development. Those roles gave me experience working on systems used by other people, beyond my own projects.

I also founded Conestoga's first cybersecurity club. With four executives, we grew it to more than 80 members and ran workshops on CTFs, Kali Linux, cryptography, and security. Teaching and troubleshooting alongside other students was a big part of what made it worth doing.

I'm continuing to work on security, networking, and software projects while studying at Guelph. You can find the builds, demos, and code on my projects page.
